FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Is this the official AO3 app?

No. AO3 Reader is an independent, unofficial application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Organization for Transformative Works or Archive of Our Own.

Can I read AO3 works offline?

Yes. Download supported works in advance and continue reading without an internet connection.

Is it available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. AO3 Reader is available through both the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Can I customize fonts and themes?

Yes. The reader includes multiple themes, dark mode, font controls, text-size options, spacing controls, and customizable layouts.
